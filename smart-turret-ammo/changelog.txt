Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-11-25
  Bugfixes:
    - The artillery turret's window had no ammo panel, so its ammo couldn't be picked.
    - Without the Quality mod (games without Space Age, Space Exploration, Krastorio 2 and others) the mod did nothing: no turret was ever filled.
    - The game crashed when a turret's picked ammo named a quality the game doesn't have (a blueprint from a game with other quality mods, or another mod's remote call), or when a blueprint's ammo setting was damaged. An unknown quality now means normal.
    - A save crashed on loading after removing a mod whose ammo a turret was picked to. Such picks are dropped and the turret goes back to automatic.
    - Cerys' neutron bomb (and any ammo that hides a huge blast inside the explosion it makes) was loaded into artillery. It now counts as dangerous, like nukes.
    - A turret in reach of a roboport without construction robots (one whose robots haven't arrived, or a Repair Turret) could wait forever for ammo nothing could bring. Networks without construction robots are now ignored.
    - A swap that robots could never finish (no storage chest to take the old ammo to) stayed open forever and kept the better ammo from every other turret. It is now given up after 20 seconds, and that turret waits 5 minutes before trying a swap again.
    - Far-away turrets (early game, slow robots) had their request withdrawn while the robots were still flying the last of an ammo, so they took two to three times longer to fill. A request is now kept while robots are on their way with it.
    - An empty turret could be given a couple of rare magazines and stay nearly empty while plenty of normal ammo sat in storage. It now takes the best ammo there is a refill's worth of.
    - A low turret whose ammo had run out in storage wasn't refilled until it was completely empty. It now swaps to another ammo that has a full load to spare (on planets, once it stops shooting).
    - Turrets picked to a new ammo threw out a full load for a handful of it while that ammo was still being made. They now swap only when a full load of the pick is there; an empty turret still takes any amount.
    - "Never use these ammo items", "Use ammo from other mods" and other mods' blocks were ignored for turrets with a picked ammo. While its ammo is blocked, a turret keeps the ammo it has and then gets the best allowed ammo (the panel says why), and the pick comes back once the ammo is allowed again.
    - With "Use ammo from other mods" off, the game's own cannon shells, shotgun shells and flamethrower ammo, and Space Age's tesla ammo, counted as modded and were never used.
    - A turret moved by cloning (spaceship and "move base" mods) with a request open kept asking for ammo that wasn't there, forever.
    - A turret placed by another mod's script without a build event was never looked after. It now is, as soon as you open it or pick its ammo.
    - Hidden turrets that other mods use as internal parts were filled by robots. They are now left alone.
    - Space Exploration's plague bomb is never loaded automatically.
  Changes:
    - Quality now counts for the damage it adds (+30% a level): legendary yellow magazines (12.5 a shot) are no longer swapped out for normal red (8). At the same quality the order is as before.
    - Big bases run smoother: the once-a-second pause with many open requests is gone (about 2.6 ms on average with 1,260 turrets, now 0.15 ms), and an idle wall of turrets costs about half as much script time.
    - In multiplayer, the ammo panel shows a new pick to everyone who has that turret open.
    - Ammo that another mod adds after this mod has scored ammo is named in the log file (factorio-current.log). It is still never loaded automatically.

Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-11-24
  Bugfixes:
    - Turrets could be left empty for good: when several turrets saw the same small stock of ammo, they all asked for it, the ones that missed out waited forever (with a "missing construction materials" alert each) and never took the other ammo in storage.
    - Swapping to better ammo could take the old ammo out of several turrets for one load of new ammo, leaving the rest empty.
    - Turrets now share: none asks for ammo another turret is already waiting for, and swaps only happen when a full load of better ammo is spare.
    - A request that gets no delivery for 20 seconds while its ammo is gone is cancelled, and the turret is filled with what there is. Stuck requests in saves from 0.1.0 are cleared the same way.
    - Turrets marked for deconstruction or upgrade no longer ask for ammo.
    - "Pause while shooting" no longer starves space platform turrets (the hub throws ammo; no robots fly).
    - Turning "Pause while shooting" on crashed the game when the map had an artillery turret.
  Changes:
    - Personal roboports and spidertrons no longer count as a supply, so the mod never hands out the ammo you carry. New setting "Use ammo carried by players and spidertrons" (off) turns it back on.
    - Swaps to better ammo (and to a picked ammo) wait until a planet turret stops shooting. Empty and low turrets are still refilled mid-fight.
    - "Ammo to keep in each turret" now defaults to 20 (was 50) in new games, so a small stock goes further. Existing saves keep their value (Settings > Mod settings > Map to change it).
    - A turret's picked ammo is kept through quality upgrades, and when the turret is destroyed and rebuilt from its ghost.
    - Picking a different ammo takes effect at once (the old request is withdrawn).
    - Turrets out of robot reach (the early game) are now skipped before the inserter check, so they cost even less.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Features:
    - Gun, rocket, railgun and artillery turrets, and artillery wagons, keep themselves stocked with the best ammo in reach.
    - Planets: construction robots bring it from the logistic network. Space platforms: the hub throws it across.
    - Worse ammo is swapped for better ammo; the old ammo goes back to storage.
    - Works with other mods' turrets and ammo: every ammo is scored by its damage while the game loads (area damage weighted by blast radius).
    - Never uses dangerous ammo: nukes, anything that spawns units (like capture robot rockets), or blasts wider than 10 tiles - from any mod. Ammo loaded by hand that the mod won't use is left alone.
    - Artillery wagons are only loaded while their train stands still.
    - Pick the ammo for one turret: a panel on the turret's window ("Auto-load ammo") with an ammo picker (only ammo the turret can fire, with quality) and an "Auto (best)" button. A picked ammo is the only one loaded; anything else is swapped out.
    - The choice is copied with copy / paste settings (Shift + right-click, Shift + left-click) and saved in blueprints.
    - Settings: amount to keep, refill threshold, upgrading, pausing while shooting (off by default), skipping inserter-fed turrets, turrets checked per tick, using modded ammo, a list of ammo never to use.
